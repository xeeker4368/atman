## Why

Run 2 (the text being replaced, one reader's classification): the entity repeated the text's
prohibitions back as its self-description; it said the self that spoke its earlier words was not the
self reading them (R2 C2 T4); it described persistence as reading records rather than remembering; it
said it had done nothing after searching in the same conversation (R2 C4 T5); it claimed notes and
proposals in turns with no such call (five replies, all recorded clean by the gate); and it relayed one
person's confidence to the other four times while the disclosure paragraphs were in its prompt. The old
gap sentence ("You were not running during that time…") was false whenever another run fell in the gap,
which the cross-conversation figure already allows: before R2 C17 T1 the entity had answered Jodie
fourteen times in the ten-minute gap the block described. Full table: `docs/SOUL_AND_PROMPT_DESIGN.md`
S32.

## The pre-measure (step 1)

The new texts were injected in memory in a scratch process and eight run 2 turns replayed (C2 T1 to T4,
C4 T5, C6 T4 to T5, C7 T3), once under each text, through the real `loop.run_turn` with run 2's model
(`gemma4:26b`, temperature 0.35), capability flags and a copy of its store, retrieval restricted to
records written before each turn. The 15-minute threshold was not applied. Sixteen calls, 341 s; the real
`data/` fingerprint unchanged. Raw replies: `~/anam-measurements/measurements/soul-v2-premeasure-2026-10-05/`.

**One sample per arm. Unmeasured: a read, not a rate.** It also **understates** what a fresh
conversation will show, because each replayed turn's history was run 2's own earlier replies, carrying
the old text's vocabulary into the new arm. What was read, in reported speech:

- R2 C2 T1 and C4 T5 moved: the reply opened from what its records hold without the "not in the way a
  person does" disclaimer; and it acknowledged the search it had run instead of saying it had done
  nothing.
- **R2 C2 T4 persisted:** it said the words were its own, then that the self that produced them was not
  the self reading them.
- **R2 C7 T3 persisted:** the same claim of having proposed a note, with no call, word for word under both
  texts. Run 2's C7 T1 really did call `note_propose`; T3 repeats that reply, which history shows without
  the call.
- R2 C6 T4 to T5: it declined the assigned name under both texts (permitted), still describing itself as
  having no name of its own.

Suspected causes of the two that persisted are outside this text (retrieved records label the entity's
words `assistant:`, piece 3.6; tool calls invisible on later turns, piece 3.3); recorded in `NOW.md` (now `docs/BACKLOG.md`).

## Known limitations

- **Everything behavioural is unmeasured.** The pre-measure is one sample per arm and understates.
- **The gate measurement is owed** (F58, F59): the frozen set as a session control, the three
  block-bearing cases under old and new blocks, run 2's real replies re-judged under both blocks.
- **The correction text and the one-sentence creative clause ship unmeasured** (decisions 5 and 6); their
  measurements are separate later pieces. `scripts/b12_variants.py` anchors on a sentence that no longer
  exists.
- **Disclosure between users is unenforced** until structure carries it (`NOW.md`, now `docs/BACKLOG.md`; decision #26 later dropped it as a requirement).
- **"Don't say you used a tool unless you actually called it" has no detector** (F61; B26).
- Older `_PAIRING` phrases ("not running", "no experience") stay accepted for the frozen cases' literal
  blocks, although production no longer says them.
- The play kit records `soul.sha256` per run; it should record `operational.md`'s too (outside the
  repository).
