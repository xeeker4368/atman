# 2026-11-10: note-pass-v2.1 (Tier 2; amended 2026-11-11)

**What changed.**
- **The pass instruction** (`note_texts.PASS_INSTRUCTION`). #27's six criteria are unchanged. New
  rules: one subject per note; revise or retire a listed note only when a person's message in this
  conversation speaks to its subject, otherwise leave it, and proposing nothing is the normal
  outcome; a revision replaces the whole note, keeping what is still true; one `quote:` line per
  message quoted. The format is now limits in prose plus four worked examples that parse, with no
  placeholder value and no `kind:` on REVISE or RETIRE. Length 3,168 of the 4,000 allowance.
- **The parser** (`note_pass.parse_block`, `parse`):
  - `quote:` may repeat;
  - at most `notes.pass_max_quote_lines` (3, new) quote lines; more is malformed, named;
  - `kind:` or `subject:` on REVISE or RETIRE is ignored, recorded in `set_aside` as
    `why = "ignored"`, `reason = "ignored field kind"` (or `subject`); other unknown fields stay malformed;
  - a reply with no block starting ADD, REVISE, RETIRE or DROP is outcome none, nothing malformed
    (a bolded `**ADD**` or a lowercase head with field lines still counts as an attempt).
- **The check** (`note_quotes.resolve_quotes`): every quote line is resolved and every fragment
  must match; the minimum applies across the block; there is an evidence entry for every
  fragment; refusals are prefixed `quote i of n:`. A closing mark, whitespace and an opening mark
  also split fragments. `scripts.note pass` prints an ignored field as "noted".
- **Caps re-derived** for three quote lines: `notes.pass_num_predict` 1,536 → 2,560 (4 × 489 + 100
  = 2,056, rounded up to 5 × 512); `notes.pass_piece_max_chars` 90,000 → 87,000 (the window it
  leaves is 87,384 characters). The instruction states the quote-line cap.

**Why.** Run 5's repass (attempt 2, read from the copy): a RETIRE in a conversation that never
mentions the note's subject; an ADD combining two subjects; two half-note REVISEs set aside for
`kind:` (one copied the placeholder); a quote joined with `" "`; a prose "No changes." recorded
as malformed.

**Tested.** Each rule's text and the examples (they parse, no placeholders, no `kind:` on
REVISE/RETIRE); parsing of repeated quotes, ignored and unknown fields, prose replies and
attempted-but-malformed replies; resolution across quote lines; the mark-pair split; three pass
tests; the ignored subject, the quote-line cap and both derivations. Mutations for rules 6 to 9
and the amendment each fail tests. Replay on a copy: C5 and C7 now resolve; C10 is none; C8
parses, its `;`-joined quote still refused.

**Known limits.** The three-line maximal block is not tokenized (no model calls); the live test
measures it. The instruction is unmeasured until run5-repass2.
