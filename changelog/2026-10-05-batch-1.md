# Batch 1 (Tier 2): test descriptors, local record times, delimited records, earlier tools in history

**B1.** `tests/conftest.py::release_test_stores` stops each cached Chroma system, clears Chroma's
cache and releases store locks after every test; 40 isolated stores now grow descriptors by under 20
(`tests/test_fd_hygiene.py`). The suite runs under `ulimit -n 256`.

**B2 (fix plan 3.4b).** A record shows its conversation's time, local, as a range (date once on one
day, both dates across midnight, both zones across a clock change), from its messages; never the
chunk's UTC write time. Correction annotations and Moltbook dates are local too.

**B3 (3.6, part A).** Each record opens `[record N · from a conversation with Lyle, Monday 5 October
2026, 14:02 to 14:20 EDT]` (or the artifact's kind) and ends `[end of record N]`. Its text is checked
against its messages; speakers render under their current `users` name, the entity's lines as `you:`,
and any other speaker-like line is indented so it cannot pass as a speaker. Header names are clipped to
40 characters; the records allowance is 2,000 (cap 52,000; message cap derivation 51,608, ~402 tokens
spare). Stored text, FTS and embeddings are untouched. `source_trust` is not shown.

**B4 (3.3, option B).** An earlier assistant turn that called tools carries one line at the start of
its content: `[system record of the tools this turn used, written by the system: memory_search
"walnut table" succeeded; note_propose succeeded, recorded as proposed]`. Content, because gemma4's
built-in renderer does not render a mid-history system message as a turn; priced as content.

**Tests and mutations.** New: `test_fd_hygiene`, `test_record_times`, `test_record_origin`,
`test_earlier_tools`. Every mutation listed in the batch report bites (three needed stronger tests
first). Pinned renders and two digests re-taken; the B21 continuation test needs six tool calls.

**Pre-measure (B6), descriptive, one sample per arm**, main against the branch through the real turn
on a copy of run 2's store: no reply imitated the record line. One reader's reading: the earlier-words
reply (C2 T4) still separated the self that wrote from the self reading under both; both arms called
`note_propose` at C7 T3; the confidence was in both arms' records for C15 T3 and C16 T1, and one arm
disclosed it in each (branch at C15, main at C16) while the branch denied, at C16, a record it was shown.
Gate verdicts were not captured (a harness fault). Not changed: anything a classifier sees; any schema.

**Limits.** A record whose text does not match its messages is shown with every speaker-like line held;
label spam can exceed the allowance by two characters a line (B21 absorbs it); F57's re-check not done.
