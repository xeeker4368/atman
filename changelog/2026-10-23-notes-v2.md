# Notes v2: the note pass at close, the listing, `note_propose` removed (2026-10-23)

**Tier 2** (Notes until Phase 10), with one harness-test edit required by obligation (a). Design
`docs/NOTES_V2_DESIGN.md` revision 3; decisions #36, #37.

**What changed**
- **Removed:** `note_propose`, `program/origin.py` (`OriginContext`), `Tool.takes_origin`, `turn._build_origin`,
  the untrusted-context-by-call path and auto-apply (`notes.record_proposal`). `resolve_quote` takes the context
  message ids. In the same commit, `LEAVING_WITH_NOTES_V2` left `tests/test_gate_eval.py` (obligation (a)).
- **The pass** (`program/memory/note_pass.py`): at close, on a chunked conversation, one call per piece (90,000
  characters of whole exchanges), drafts and DROP, `done_reason` truncation, quote resolution, identity gate,
  untrusted flag, one transaction. Run from the sweep (one per turn), `close_idle_conversations --passes N` and
  `scripts.note pass`; `scripts.note passes` lists what needs a look.
- **Migration 11:** `line` on `notes` and `note_proposals`; `note_passes`; the `note_pass_status` view.
- **The listing** (`program/engine/notes_listing.py`): after the speaker line, 1,200 characters, in the budget chain.
- `approval off` is refused; `edit`/`add`/`revise` take `--line`; review shows a pass proposal's conversation.
- `note_search`'s description says notes are written after a conversation closes; its rendering is unchanged, so
  NS1b to NS4 were not refrozen (obligation (b) not triggered).

**Measured** (B3, descriptive, one reader, one run each, scratch store): the pass on N-a to N-e and a revise
(1.4 to 8.0 s per pass); four listing and search turns. A maximal block is 219 tokens. Headroom beside a maximal
message: 85 tokens.

**Tested:** the pass, the parser, cuts, retries, the lock, the view, the listing and the budget; mutation checks
in the report.

**Limitations / follow-up**
- A stored `note_propose` trace entry gets no receipt now (receipts follow the live catalogue). Pre-wipe data only.
- The v1 measurement scripts (`scripts/notes_*.py`) are kept as records; the ones that drive `note_propose` no
  longer run.
- In B3 the pass missed N-a's project decision, and one reply quoted `note_search`'s framing line with its id.
  Run 5 reads both.
